Good to know

The questions we get asked most, answered plainly.

How far ahead should we book an Asian wedding DJ?

Peak Saturdays in summer go twelve to eighteen months ahead. We can run more than one wedding on the same date, but popular dates still fill up early.

Do you cover the ceremony and the reception if they're in different places?

Yes. We can run two crews on the same day, so one team covers the ceremony while another sets up the reception. Both are Euphoria crews, briefed in the same planning meetings.

Can we choose the music, or does the DJ decide?

Both. You give us the must-plays, the do-not-plays and the family songs that matter, and we build the structure around them. The rest is read live — a floor doesn't follow a spreadsheet.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Regularly — Italy, Spain, Greece, India, Dubai and Mexico so far. We either fly out with control kit to play on a trusted local rig, or ship the full production, and arrive two days before.

How much does a production package cost?

Every wedding is quoted individually after a short consultation, because your venue, guest numbers and the scale of production you want all shape the answer.

What happens if a DJ is unavailable on the day?

With a team of eighteen — including three resident DJs — nobody's booking depends on a single person's availability. Your planning is documented and shared across the team from the first consultation.
